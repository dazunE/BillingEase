Why BillingEase exists

Ines Alvarez ran a six-person design studio in Red Hook for nine years. Every month she lost a Sunday to the books. Her software wanted to talk about accounts receivable, accrual basis and unapplied credits. She wanted to know three things: what’s coming in, what’s going out, and what’s hers to keep.

Theo Park had spent six years building payment systems for banks. Over breakfast on Atlantic Avenue in January 2023, he drew those three numbers on a napkin and asked: what if the whole app was just that, and everything else lived behind one of them?

That napkin is framed in our office. Every feature we’ve shipped since (invoices, receipts, payroll, sales tax, the full books for your accountant) had to answer one question first: which number does this help you understand?
