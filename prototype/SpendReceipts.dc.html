Kept for tax

Each receipt stays attached to its charge in your books. If you’re ever asked to show what an expense was for, it’s one click away.

We keep them for at least seven years after the tax year, even if you close your account, and you can download them all whenever you like.

How receipts count

  • Card receipts don’t add anything: the charge is already in Going out.
  • Cash receipts add their total to Going out, because no bank charge will ever show up.
  • Tax on receipts is noted so your sales tax and deductions are right in Taxes.
