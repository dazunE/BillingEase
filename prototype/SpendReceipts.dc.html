Kept for tax

Each receipt stays attached to its charge in your books. If you’re ever asked to show what an expense was for, it’s one click away.

We keep them for at least seven years after the tax year, even if you close your account.

How receipts count

  • A charge that’s already sorted doesn’t change: it’s in Going out. The receipt is the proof.
  • A charge we hadn’t sorted joins Going out once its receipt gives it a category.
  • No charge at all (cash, a personal card): the receipt becomes its own expense and adds to Going out.
  • Tax on receipts is noted so your deductions are right in Taxes.
