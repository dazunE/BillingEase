Put 2025 away

Year-end close · lock date
{{ye.badge}}

Nothing to put away yet. Your first year ends Dec 31, 2026; when it does, a short checklist shows up here so you can hand the year to your accountant and lock it.

Once your accountant has filed for 2025, lock it. Nothing dated on or before December 31, 2025 can be added, changed or deleted, so the numbers on your tax return always match your books. Only you or your accountant can unlock it, and we note who did and when.
