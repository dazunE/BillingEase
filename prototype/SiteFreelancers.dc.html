Quarterly estimated taxes

Tax four times a year, explained simply.

When you work for yourself, nobody takes tax out of your pay. So the IRS asks you to send some of it four times a year, as you earn, instead of all at once in April.

  • 1Every payment is split as it lands. A share goes into your tax set-aside, so you never spend it by accident.
  • 2Two weeks before each due date, we remind you with the amount to send and how to send it.
  • 3In April, the hard part is done. Your accountant gets the year’s books in one click.
A guide, not tax advice. Your accountant can set your exact rate in BillingEase.
