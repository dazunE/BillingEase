Why we do this

When you work for yourself, nobody takes tax out of your pay. The IRS expects it in four payments a year instead. Each month we move a share of your profit into a separate tax pot, so when the payment is due the money is already waiting. 25% is a common starting point; your accountant can tell you if yours should be higher or lower. Changing it here changes Yours to keep everywhere.
