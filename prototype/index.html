Coming in−Going out=Yours to keep

One app, two countries. A business picks its country when it signs up, and that decides the currency, the tax year, the expense categories, invoice wording, payroll and what your accountant sees. Every screen shares one set of data: add an invoice and it shows in the list, on the customer and in your three numbers.

Try the full flow

  1. Sign up, pick the UK or Sri Lanka, answer the setup questions, and choose the sample business or start empty.
  2. Send an invoice from New. Watch Coming in grow, then find it in All invoices and on the customer. In Sri Lanka it’s a Tax Invoice with VAT and SSCL lines.
  3. Add an expense with an attachment, mark a bill paid. UK: check the quarter’s readiness and send it to your accountant. Sri Lanka: run October payroll and open a payslip.
  4. Answer a request from your accountant, see what they see, and ask a tax question.
  5. Sign out and back in, or accept an accountant invite as a new client.
