BillingEase

The whole app, clickable end to end

Coming in−Going out=Yours to keep

Every screen shares one set of data. Send an invoice and it shows up in the invoice list, on the customer and in your three numbers. Pay a bill and Going out moves. Start on the landing page and use it like a new customer would, or jump to any screen below.

Start on the landing page Open Home with the sample month Open as a brand new business Reset sample data

The sample is Northwind Studio, a design studio in Brooklyn, on Sunday, Oct 4, 2026. What you change is saved in this browser only. Use “Reset sample data” (also in the account menu under the MC avatar) to start October over.

Try the full flow

  1. Sign up, answer the setup questions, and choose a sample month or an empty business.
  2. Bill someone from New. Watch Coming in grow, then find the invoice in All invoices and on the customer.
  3. Record a payment, scan a receipt, pay a bill and approve payroll. Watch Going out and Yours to keep move.
  4. Accept a quote and turn it into an invoice. Give a customer money back.
  5. Rename your business, invite someone, switch to your other business and back.
