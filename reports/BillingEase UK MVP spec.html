BillingEase MVP for the UK and Sri Lanka

Manual-entry bookkeeping, invoicing and payroll for small businesses, with the country chosen at sign-up deciding the tax rules, reports, payroll depth and the accountant workflow. In the UK it is the client app for MTD-ready practices. In Sri Lanka it is the everyday books behind a TaxWise.lk return, sold through practitioners and direct.

1. One product, two countries

One codebase and one design. A business belongs to one country, chosen at sign-up, and a country pack switches the parts that differ: currency, tax year, tax rates, expense categories, invoice wording, payroll rules, reports and deadlines. Everything else (the three numbers, invoices, quotes, expenses, bills, the accountant link) is shared.

UK Who it's forSole traders and landlords entering Making Tax Digital for Income Tax (£30k wave April 2027, £20k wave April 2028), sold mainly through accountants on the TaxWise practitioner tool.
UK The jobKeep records in HMRC's categories every quarter; the practice files through its own software.
LK Who it's forSmall businesses and sole proprietors, through TaxWise.lk practitioners and by direct sign-up.
LK The jobKeep the books, issue Sri Lankan tax invoices, run payroll with EPF, ETF and APIT, and hand the year to TaxWise.lk for the income tax return.

Why no filing in either country yet: HMRC has paused new MTD Income Tax software access until at least early 2027, and Sri Lanka's IRD e-invoicing link to RAMIS is still in pilot (from 1 May 2026, full rollout targeted for end-2026). Both filing links come later, once the books are in use.

3. Shared core Both

  • Three numbers: Coming in − Going out = Yours to keep, where "Yours to keep" is profit after the estimated tax for that country.
  • Invoices and quotes: one-sentence composer, full editor, PDF, email, repeating drafts, money back (credit notes), record payments by hand, mark a quote accepted and turn it into an invoice.
  • Expenses: manual entry, country categories, business-use percentage, attachments (photo or PDF, no automatic reading), every-month templates.
  • Bills and suppliers: enter a bill, mark paid in full or part.
  • Customers and products: customer list and detail, products and services with prices (no stock).
  • Books: append-only ledger, categories, manual journal entries for practitioners, check against a bank statement by hand, CSV statement import.
  • Reports: profit and loss, balance sheet, aged receivables and payables, tax summaries for the country, CSV export.
  • Accountant link, Ask, notifications, help, security and data export.

4. United Kingdom UK

Unchanged from draft 1 in substance; summarised here.

Records in HMRC's categories

  • Expense categories map one-to-one to the self-employment boxes and MTD categories (cost of goods, staff costs, car, van and travel, premises, repairs, office costs, advertising and entertainment, interest, bank charges, bad debts, professional fees, depreciation (not allowable), other). Plain names on screen, HMRC names underneath.
  • Cash basis by default; allowable or not badges; business-use split; consolidated expenses for users under the VAT threshold; mileage log at 45p then 25p per mile.

MTD quarters

  • Quarter card on Home with income, expenses, gaps and the practice's filing date; readiness checks; quarter summary by category; "Send to accountant" with a CSV in the practice's agreed layout; lock once filed.

Landlord mode

  • Properties with ownership share, rent roll, property categories, residential finance costs kept separate for the basic-rate reduction, the £1,000 property allowance when it is better.

Payroll records

  • Employees and pay runs entered from the payroll software or a CSV; staff costs and the HMRC liability posted to the books; PAYE due on the 22nd as a reminder; a banner on every payroll screen: "Run payroll and report to HMRC through your payroll software."

Tax estimate

  • Income Tax (Scottish switch) plus Class 4 NI, landlord finance-cost reduction, other income added by the user, payments on account and a monthly set-aside.

The examples show layout only. How SSCL sits in the VAT base and the APIT figure must be confirmed against IRD guidance before build (see section 15).

Tax invoices, VAT and SSCL

  • Business profile holds the TIN and VAT registration number; registered businesses issue invoices titled "Tax Invoice" in the revised IRD format that has been mandatory since 1 July 2026.
  • VAT at 18% by default, with zero-rated and exempt options, and 20.5% available for financial services from 1 July 2026.
  • SSCL at 2.5% as its own line and its own summary, because it is a separate levy with no input credit.
  • VAT and SSCL period summaries (output tax, input tax from recorded bills, net payable) laid out in the order of the IRD return, for the business or practitioner to enter. No RAMIS connection in the MVP.
  • Registration threshold reminder: BillingEase watches turnover and warns as it approaches the VAT threshold (the exact figure is disputed between LKR 60M and 36M a year; see section 15).

Payroll (full calculation, no e-filing)

  • Employees: name, NIC, EPF number, start date, basic salary, fixed allowances, bank details for the payment list.
  • Monthly pay run: earnings (basic, allowances, overtime), EPF employee 8% and employer 12% and ETF 3% on EPF-liable earnings, APIT from the current tax tables (personal relief of LKR 1.8M a year since 1 April 2025), net pay.
  • Payslips: PDF in English, emailed or downloaded; Sinhala and Tamil later.
  • Schedules to file yourself: monthly EPF and ETF contribution schedules (C-form style), APIT schedule, bank salary transfer list. Each pay run posts salaries, EPF, ETF and APIT liabilities to the books.
  • Reminders: contribution and APIT payment dates on Home.
  • Year-end: APIT totals per employee and employer certificates for the TaxWise.lk return.
  • Label: "BillingEase calculates payroll. Pay EPF, ETF and APIT and file returns through the EPF, ETF and IRD channels."

Income tax estimate and TaxWise.lk hand-off

  • "Yours to keep" estimates income tax on business profit for the year of assessment using the current rates and reliefs, and shows quarterly instalment dates.
  • At year end, "Send to TaxWise.lk" moves business income, expenses by schedule line, capital allowance items, VAT/SSCL totals and payroll totals into the user's TaxWise.lk return, or into the practitioner's file for that client.
  • Users who also use TaxWise.lk sign in once; the two products share the account.

Getting paid and bank statements

  • Record payments by hand: bank transfer, cheque (with cheque number and date), cash, LankaQR.
  • CSV statement import with saved column templates for the main banks; PDF statement reading after launch. Sri Lanka has no open banking rail, so no bank feeds.
  • PayHere payment links can come after the MVP; it is the CBSL-approved payment aggregator.

Two channels

  • Through practitioners: a TaxWise.lk practitioner invites clients; the practitioner sees every client's books, sends requests and receives the year-end hand-off.
  • Direct: a business signs up alone, keeps its books and runs payroll, and can buy a TaxWise.lk return or invite a practitioner at any time.

7. Ask (AI answers) Both

  • The TaxWise AI engine answers in the app, grounded per country: HMRC manuals, gov.uk and UK legislation for the UK; the Inland Revenue Act, IRD guidance and gazettes, and EPF/ETF rules for Sri Lanka.
  • Clearly marked as AI, cites its sources, never files anything, and offers "Ask your accountant" when the question needs judgement. In the UK it follows HMRC's January 2026 guidance on generative AI in tax software.

10. How country rules work

  • Each country is a country pack: currency and formats, tax year, categories and their mapping, tax rates with start and end dates, invoice template rules, payroll rules and tables, deadline calendar, report layouts, Ask sources.
  • Rates and thresholds live in dated data tables, never in code, so a gazette or Budget change is a data update. Sri Lanka changed VAT, SSCL and APIT rules several times in 2024–2026; the UK changes bands every April.
  • Screens check the pack to show or hide features (landlord mode, mileage, quarters, VAT/SSCL periods, payroll depth).
  • A business's country is fixed after sign-up; moving country means a new business.
  • Adding a third country later means a new pack plus its practitioner channel.

11. Marketing site

  • Country chosen from the first visit (from the browser locale, changeable in the header): billingease.co.uk style UK pages and Sri Lanka pages, or one domain with /uk and /lk.
  • UK: MTD for Income Tax explained, For landlords, For accountants as the lead page, Ask, pricing in £ with a per-client practice price.
  • Sri Lanka: VAT, SSCL and tax invoices explained, Payroll with EPF/ETF/APIT, For practitioners, "Works with TaxWise.lk", pricing in Rs for direct sign-ups and practitioners.
  • Drop or hide: Outside the US, Shops and makers (stock), the US-oriented Teams page, Switch to BillingEase until import exists, partner tiers until set, unverified security certifications.

14. Open decisions

  1. Name. Trademark checks for "BillingEase" in the UK, EU and Sri Lanka, and whether the UK practice tool and BillingEase share one brand.
  2. Pricing. UK: per-client practice price and a direct price. Sri Lanka: direct monthly price in Rs, a practitioner price, and a bundle with a TaxWise.lk return.
  3. Sri Lanka companies. Books only for companies in the MVP, or also corporate income tax figures for the practitioner.
  4. Payroll edge cases in Sri Lanka. Which allowances count for EPF, bonuses and arrears, terminal benefits (gratuity) records.
  5. Export formats. Which filing software the first UK practices use; which bank CSV formats to support first in Sri Lanka.
  6. Hosting. One region for both countries, or separate regions.

15. Facts to verify before build

LK Sri Lanka

  • VAT registration threshold (sources conflict: LKR 60M or 36M a year) and the quarterly test.
  • Whether SSCL is included in the VAT base on a tax invoice, and the exact revised invoice fields required since 1 July 2026.
  • RAMIS e-invoicing API status, technical spec and which businesses must connect, and when.
  • Current APIT tables, tax rates and reliefs for 2026/27 and the treatment of allowances and bonuses.
  • EPF/ETF contribution base, return formats, payment and filing deadlines, and any e-return channel.
  • Income tax instalment dates and rates for individuals and partnerships, and business income schedule lines in the return.
  • Record retention period and PDPA cross-border transfer rules.

UK United Kingdom

  • MTD quarterly deadlines and the calendar-quarter election; MTD self-employment and property category names for 2026/27.
  • Consolidated-expenses rule and the VAT registration threshold.
  • 2026/27 Income Tax bands (including Scottish), Class 4 NI, payments on account tests.
  • Simplified expense rates, trading and property allowances, cash basis rules for landlords.
  • Record-keeping periods; RTI and payslip rules that payroll records must not appear to replace.
